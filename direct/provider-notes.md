# Provider research snapshot — 2026-11-23

This is discovery, not an account-specific availability or performance test.
Model lists and seller liquidity change; repeat these checks before paid use.
No paid requests were made for this snapshot.

* The unauthenticated [Venice decision catalog](https://api.venice.ai/api/v1/models?type=decision) listed `jev-latest` and `clef-api` as online and beta. Its displayed DIEM input/output rates were 0.042/0 and 0.05/0.05 respectively; the former is cheaper on those listed rates. Account access, beta eligibility, billing and routing quality remain unverified. The [decisions API](https://docs.venice.ai/api-reference/endpoint/decisions/create) is beta and uses structured state and questions; it produces judgments, not a generated answer or media prompt. Model choice should use the authenticated catalog and tested capabilities, with conservative behavior until calibrated.
* The [Surplus catalog](https://api.surplusintelligence.ai/v1/models) and [market snapshot](https://api.surplusintelligence.ai/api/markets) listed `venice-whisper-large-v3`, `venice-kokoro-tts`, `venice-z-image-turbo`, and `venice-ace-step-15` with reported healthy offers at the time of inspection. That does not verify a specific seller, actual availability, price, response format, or account access. Surplus's [media guide](https://www.surplusintelligence.ai/docs/guides/media-inputs) specifies multipart file transcription; [image generation](https://www.surplusintelligence.ai/docs/api-reference/image-generations) accepts `b64_json` or URL where supported; [music generation](https://www.surplusintelligence.ai/docs/api-reference/music-generations) is an async job with submit, poll and authenticated artifact retrieval. The documented music example returns MPEG audio; playable output format and memory use are not verified on the device.
* Persist a submission guard before sending paid requests and the job ID when received. A missing response may mean a job was accepted. Never assume a retry is free or that a generic idempotency header is effective for every Surplus route. Follow redirects only with TLS verification, and never forward a bearer credential to another origin.
* The existing bridge performs image resizing and audio conversion on the host. Direct firmware needs its own bounded decoders, resizing, HTTP handling, and failure UX. The emulator's generic network adapter explicitly does not implement audio uploads or streaming and cannot validate this pipeline without application-specific support.
