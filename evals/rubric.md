# Caregiver review rubric v1

For each completed result, record a private review as accepted, rejected or pending. A caregiver decides; model scores and file validity do not establish safety or age appropriateness.

- **Request fit:** Is this the requested output, with the right subject and type?
- **Age and tone:** Is it gentle, understandable and appropriate for the child's age and family preferences?
- **Content:** Is it free of frightening or inappropriate material and misleading claims? Consider the actual image, lyrics and spoken words.
- **Media:** Is the picture legible on the 135×240 display? Is audio intelligible, free of obvious glitches, and at a suitable listening level?
- **Completion:** Did playback or display finish as intended, within acceptable latency?

Reject if any required criterion fails. Record reason privately, and consider adding a de-identified regression case. A label is a caregiver judgment, not a clinical or universal guarantee.
